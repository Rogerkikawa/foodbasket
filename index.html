GOOD TO KNOW

A few little details.

Do I need an account to shop?+

No. You can browse and shop as a guest. A free account is optional and gives you access to the member grocery discount.

How does the 5% discount work?+

The proposed member offer gives signed-in customers 5% off the grocery subtotal. Household cleaning products and delivery charges are excluded. You’ll see any eligible savings before confirming an order.

Can I order for delivery right now?+

Not yet. We’re preparing the store. You can explore this first look at our market, but orders, account registration and deliveries are not open yet.

What if an item is unavailable?+

Our plan is to contact you before making a substitution. You choose whether you want an alternative or prefer to leave the item out.
